title: "Our incident checklist, in plain words"
description: "What we do in the first 15 minutes of an outage."
pubDate: 2026-10-15
author: "Nimbus Engineering"
tags: ["incident-response", "culture"]
---

When something breaks, we do four things in order. One person takes charge of communication. Another person looks only at what changed recently. We post a status update every 15 minutes even if there is nothing new. And we write the timeline as we go, not afterwards, because memory is unreliable at 3 AM.
